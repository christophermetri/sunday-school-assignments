Auxiliary Servants

Auxiliary servants are those who, due to studies, living or studying away from the church, or other life circumstances, may not be able to commit to regular weekly service. They remain part of our servants community and are always welcome to serve during Spiritual Days, Mahragan, Summer Camp, retreats, and other special events. Auxiliary servants are also servants who have had less than 50% attendance to the service.
